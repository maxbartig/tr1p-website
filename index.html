HOW IT WORKS

Zero effort. Just drive.

◉
STEP 1
Drive like normal.

TR1P notices when you start driving and records the route in the background. No buttons, and no need to touch your phone.

▪
STEP 2
Park.

Once you've been parked for a few minutes, the trip ends. Stoplights and traffic won't split it in two.

+3
STEP 3
See how it compared.

TR1P checks the fastest-route estimate for the same trip and shows the time saved or added, plus your route, distance, and stats.
