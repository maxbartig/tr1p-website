HOW IT WORKS

Zero effort. The race starts when you pull out.

◉
STEP 1
Pull out. That’s it.

Auto-detection uses the motion chip to spot the drive and snapshot the map’s predicted ETA for your route — no buttons, no setup.

▪
STEP 2
Race the ETA, live.

Your progress vs the prediction runs on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. Ahead shows green. Behind just counts as a drive — no penalty.

+4
STEP 3
Park. Bank the minutes.

Arrive early and the difference goes in the bank the second you park. Streaks, records, and your passport update automatically.
